# Sample data

`src/data/sample-history.json` is a deterministic, fictional listening
history generated by `tools/generate-sample-data.py` (seed `20261118`):

- 3,895 plays, 41 fictional artists, 123 tracks, 12 genres
- Nov 2025 – Nov 2026 (13 calendar months)
- ~10,877 minutes total
- Listening is heavier on weekends and evenings (peak ~21:00), with a
  summer bump — matching typical real-world patterns.

Per-month slices live in `src/data/months/YYYY-MM.json`. To regenerate:

```bash
python3 tools/generate-sample-data.py
```

All artist and track names are invented. No real person or Spotify data is
in this dataset.
