# Listener profiles

Besides the default year, TuneRewind ships three alternate fictional
listeners so the stats and Sound City can be explored from different angles:

- **Night Owl** — mostly 21:00–02:00, Electronic and Lo-Fi heavy
- **Jazz Devotee** — mornings and evenings, Jazz and Classical, long sessions
- **Genre Explorer** — flat across all 12 genres, no dominant artist

Generated by `tools/generate-profiles.py` into `src/data/profiles/*.json`
and loaded by `src/data/profiles.ts`. Switch profiles with the picker at
the top of the app — the whole page (stats, clock, skyline, share cards)
recomputes from the selected history.
